Lehi marketing agencies: 35 of 309, by service, size and rate

Lehi has the second most marketing agencies in Utah after Salt Lake City. 35 of the 309 agencies in the Utah Marketing Agencies 2026 dataset (11%) are based there. You can browse them in Lehi marketing agencies in the Brand Captain directory. This page compares those 35 with the state as a whole.

What Lehi agencies offer

Lehi is a search marketing town. SEO, Google Ads and local SEO are all more common here than statewide, and brand and full-service work are much less common.

  • SEO: 28 of 35 (80%), against 71% statewide
  • Google Ads: 21 (60%), against 53%
  • Local SEO, called out by name: 12 (34%), against 24%
  • Meta or Facebook ads: 9 (26%), against 19%
  • Ecommerce: 13 (37%), against 29%
  • Branding: 8 (23%), against 43%
  • Full service: 2 (6%), against 19%
  • Video: 5 (14%), against 21%

Web design (22, or 63%), social media (18, 51%) and email (8, 23%) sit a few points under the statewide share. The median Lehi agency names 5 of the 13 services, the same as the rest of Utah.

If you want a brand identity or a full-service shop, Salt Lake City has far more options: 41 of its 70 agencies offer branding.

Team size

31 Lehi agencies list a team size. 22 of them (71%) have fewer than 50 people, against 86% statewide. 9 list 50 or more, a high count for a city with 35 agencies. Scorpion is the largest, at 250 to 999 people. The other larger firms, such as 97th Floor and Boostability, list 50 to 249.

Age

Lehi agencies are younger than average. 30 list a founding year, and 17 of those (57%) started in 2015 or later. The median falls between 2017 and 2018, against 2014 for the state. Only 2 started before 2000: Manta (1996) and MyAdvice (1998).

What they charge

18 Lehi agencies publish an hourly rate band. The median starting rate is $100 an hour, the same as statewide. 12 of the 18 start at $100 or more, and 6 start at $150 or more. The most common band is $100 to $149 an hour (6 agencies). 2 start under $50.

21 publish a minimum project size. "$1,000+" is the most common (11 agencies), then "$5,000+" (4). Only 1 asks for $10,000 or more.

Industries

21 list the industries they serve. Home services leads with 12, then ecommerce (8), healthcare (6), SaaS (4) and dental (4). 3 name franchise brands.

How the data was built

Candidates came from Clutch, DesignRush and the BBB plus a hand-built list. Dead sites, non-Utah firms and video-only shops were removed. Services are matched from each agency's own homepage text.
