Provo marketing agencies: 26 of 309, by service, size and rate

Provo is the third largest agency city in Utah, behind Salt Lake City and Lehi. 26 of the 309 agencies in the Utah Marketing Agencies 2026 dataset (8%) are based there. You can browse them in Provo marketing agencies in the Brand Captain directory. This page compares those 26 with the state as a whole.

What Provo agencies offer

Provo agencies are more specialized than the rest of Utah. The median Provo agency names 3 or 4 of the 13 services we track, against 5 statewide. 9 of the 26 name only one or two.

Video is the one service Provo has more of than the state. Paid search, local SEO, ecommerce and email are all less common.

  • Video: 8 of 26 (31%), against 21% statewide
  • Google Ads: 10 (38%), against 53%
  • Local SEO, called out by name: 3 (12%), against 24%
  • Ecommerce: 4 (15%), against 29%
  • Email: 4 (15%), against 28%
  • Web design: 15 (58%), against 68%

SEO (18, or 69%), social media (14, 54%), Meta or Facebook ads (5, 19%) and PR (2, 8%) are close to the statewide share. Branding is a little lower at 10 (38%) against 43%.

If you need paid search, nearby Lehi has twice as many Google Ads agencies (21) as Provo (10).

Team size

25 Provo agencies list a team size, and all 25 have fewer than 50 people. 11 list 10 to 49, 9 list 2 to 9, and 5 say "under 49". Statewide, 38 agencies list 50 or more, and none of them are in Provo.

Age

25 list a founding year. The median is 2015, against 2014 for the state, and 13 (52%) started in 2015 or later. 3 started before 2000. The oldest are Hales Creative (1982) and The Walton Group (1985).

What they charge

Provo agencies that publish a rate charge more than the state average. 13 publish an hourly rate band, and their median starting rate is $150 an hour, against $100 statewide. 11 of the 13 start at $100 or more, and 8 start at $150 or more. None start under $50.

14 publish a minimum project size. "$1,000+" is the most common (5 agencies), then "$1,000 to $10,000" (4) and "$5,000+" (3). 1 asks for $10,000 to $25,000.

Industries

Only 9 Provo agencies list the industries they serve, so there is little to compare. Ecommerce, nonprofit, home services and financial services each appear twice.

How the data was built

Candidates came from Clutch, DesignRush and the BBB plus a hand-built list. Dead sites, non-Utah firms and video-only shops were removed. Services are matched from each agency's own homepage text.
