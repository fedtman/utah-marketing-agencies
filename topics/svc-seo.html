Utah SEO agencies: 219 of 309, by city, size and rate

SEO is the most common service among Utah marketing agencies. Of the 309 agencies in the Utah Marketing Agencies 2026 dataset, 219 (71%) name SEO on their own homepage. You can search them by city in Utah SEO agencies in the Brand Captain directory. This page looks at what those 219 have in common.

Where they are

The 219 SEO agencies are spread across 45 cities. Salt Lake City has the most with 42, then Lehi (28), Provo (18), Orem (11), Sandy (10) and South Jordan (10). Lehi leans harder on SEO than the capital does: 28 of its 35 agencies (80%) offer it, compared with 42 of 70 (60%) in Salt Lake City.

What comes bundled with SEO

SEO agencies sell paid search far more often than the rest of the market. 151 of the 219 (69%) also name Google Ads. Among the 90 agencies that do not name SEO, only 14 offer Google Ads. If you want one firm to run both organic and paid search, nearly every candidate will come from the SEO group.

Other services that show up next to SEO:

  • Web design: 163 (74%), against 68% statewide
  • Social media: 129 (59%)
  • Content marketing: 97 (44%)
  • Local SEO, called out by name: 73 (33%)
  • Email: 72 (33%)
  • Ecommerce: 69 (32%)
  • Meta or Facebook ads: 53 (24%)

118 agencies offer SEO, Google Ads and web design together. The median SEO agency names 5 of the 13 services we track, the same as the market overall.

How big they are

198 of the SEO agencies list a team size. 171 of those (86%) have fewer than 50 people. The two biggest groups are 10 to 49 people (63 agencies) and 2 to 9 people (60). 5 are freelancers.

How long they have been around

195 list a founding year. The median is 2014, and 95 of them (49%) started in 2015 or later. The oldest two, MāHK Advertising and ZipLocal, both date to 1971.

What they charge

119 SEO agencies publish an hourly rate band. The median starting rate is $100 an hour. 78 (66%) start at $100 or more and 32 (27%) start at $150 or more. The most common band is $100 to $149 an hour (37 agencies). 13 start under $50.

137 publish a minimum project size. "$1,000+" is the most common (62 agencies), then "$1,000 to $10,000" (37) and "$5,000+" (18). 10 list a minimum under $1,000.

Industries they name

130 SEO agencies list the industries they serve. Home services leads with 63, then ecommerce (52), healthcare (32), dental (21) and legal (18).

How the data was built

Candidates came from Clutch, DesignRush and the BBB plus a hand-built list. Dead sites, non-Utah firms and video-only shops were removed. Services are matched from each agency's own homepage text, so an agency that does SEO but never says so on its homepage is not counted here.
