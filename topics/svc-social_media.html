Utah social media agencies: 163 of 309, by city, size and rate

Social media management is offered by just over half of Utah marketing agencies. 163 of the 309 agencies in the Utah Marketing Agencies 2026 dataset (53%) name it on their homepage. You can search them by city in Utah social media agencies in the Brand Captain directory. This page covers what those 163 look like as a group and how they compare with the state.

Where they are

The 163 are spread across 37 cities. Salt Lake City has 36, then Lehi (18), Provo (14), Sandy (9) and South Jordan (8). Park City, Orem and St. George have 6 each.

The share changes by city. In Park City and South Jordan, 67% of agencies offer social media. In Salt Lake City and Lehi it is 51%, and in Orem it is 43%.

Social media agencies sell more services

The median social media agency names 6 of the 13 services we track, against 5 statewide. Only 3 sell social media and nothing else. The services that show up most often next to it:

  • SEO: 129 (79%), against 71% statewide
  • Web design: 120 (74%), against 68%
  • Google Ads: 115 (71%), against 53%
  • Content marketing: 78 (48%), against 37%
  • Email: 70 (43%), against 28%
  • Meta or Facebook ads: 47 (29%), against 19%

Paid social is the gap to ask about. Only 47 of the 163 name Meta or Facebook ads, so most of these agencies talk about posting and community management, not ad buying. If you want someone to run paid campaigns on Facebook and Instagram, ask for examples of ad accounts they manage.

Team size

155 list a team size. 131 (85%) have fewer than 50 people. The biggest groups are 10 to 49 people (57 agencies) and 2 to 9 people (51). 24 list 50 or more, which is 24 of the 35 agencies in the state that size.

Age

149 list a founding year. The median is 2014, the same as the state, and 72 (48%) started in 2015 or later. 13 started before 2000. The oldest, MāHK Advertising and ZipLocal, date to 1971.

What they charge

81 social media agencies publish an hourly rate band. The median starting rate is $100 an hour, the same as statewide. 56 (69%) start at $100 or more and 21 (26%) start at $150 or more. 8 start under $50. The most common band is $100 to $149 an hour (31 agencies), then $150 to $199 (15) and $50 to $99 (13).

92 publish a minimum project size. "$1,000+" is the most common (42), then "$5,000+" (22) and "$1,000 to $10,000" (17). 6 take projects under $1,000.

Industries

88 list the industries they serve. Home services leads with 43, then ecommerce (37), healthcare (25), dental (13) and real estate (11).

How the data was built

Candidates came from Clutch, DesignRush and the BBB plus a hand-built list. Dead sites, non-Utah firms and video-only shops were removed. Services are matched from each agency's own homepage text, so an agency that runs social accounts but never says so on its homepage is not counted.
