Utah branding agencies: 132 of 309, by city, size and rate

132 of the 309 agencies in the Utah Marketing Agencies 2026 dataset (43%) name branding on their homepage. You can search them by city in Utah branding agencies in the Brand Captain directory. This page covers what those 132 look like as a group and how they differ from the rest of the state.

Where they are

The 132 are spread across 38 cities, but Salt Lake City holds 41 of them, almost a third. Provo has 10, Lehi 8, Sandy 6 and Orem 5.

Salt Lake City is also where branding is most common. 41 of its 70 agencies (59%) offer it. In Lehi, the second largest agency city, only 8 of 35 (23%) do. Lehi agencies lean toward search and paid ads instead.

Branding comes with a website

80% of branding agencies also build websites, against 68% statewide. They are less likely to sell search and paid ads:

  • Web design: 106 (80%), against 68% statewide
  • SEO: 86 (65%), against 71%
  • Social media: 74 (56%), against 53%
  • Google Ads: 63 (48%), against 53%
  • Local SEO, called out by name: 22 (17%), against 24%
  • Video: 34 (26%), against 21%
  • PR: 18 (14%), against 9%

The median branding agency names 5 of the 13 services we track, the same as the state. Only 3 sell branding and nothing else, but 15 name just one or two services. Those are mostly design studios. If you need a logo and a website, a branding agency fits. If you also need ongoing search or ad work, check that they do it in house.

Team size

126 list a team size. 112 (89%) have fewer than 50 people. The biggest group is 2 to 9 people (44 agencies), then 10 to 49 (40). Statewide, the 2 to 9 and 10 to 49 groups are close to even, so branding shops run a little smaller.

Age

120 list a founding year. The median is 2012, two years older than the statewide median of 2014. 13 started before 2000. The oldest are R&R Partners (1974) and Hales Creative (1982). 51 (43%) started in 2015 or later.

What they charge

64 branding agencies publish an hourly rate band. The median starting rate is $100 an hour, the same as statewide. 47 (73%) start at $100 or more and 20 (31%) start at $150 or more. The most common band is $100 to $149 an hour (23 agencies), then $150 to $199 (11).

73 publish a minimum project size. "$1,000+" is the most common (24), then "$1,000 to $10,000" (20) and "$5,000+" (12). 7 ask for $25,000 or more, and 4 take projects under $1,000.

Industries

78 list the industries they serve. Ecommerce leads with 31, then home services (27), healthcare (17), and dental and financial services (9 each). Statewide, home services is first, so branding agencies skew toward product companies.

How the data was built

Candidates came from Clutch, DesignRush and the BBB plus a hand-built list. Dead sites, non-Utah firms and video-only shops were removed. Services are matched from each agency's own homepage text, so an agency that does brand work but never says so on its homepage is not counted.
