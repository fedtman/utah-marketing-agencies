Utah web design agencies: 209 of 309, by city, size and rate

Web design is the second most common service among Utah marketing agencies, just behind SEO. 209 of the 309 agencies in the Utah Marketing Agencies 2026 dataset (68%) name it on their homepage. You can search them by city in Utah web design agencies in the Brand Captain directory. This page covers what those 209 look like as a group.

Where they are

The 209 are spread across 42 cities. Salt Lake City has 49, then Lehi (22), Provo (15), South Jordan (11), Orem (8) and Sandy (8). South Jordan stands out: 11 of its 12 agencies build websites.

Almost nobody sells only websites

Only 3 of the 209 name web design and nothing else. Most sell a site as part of a marketing package:

  • SEO: 163 (78%), against 71% statewide
  • Google Ads: 122 (58%)
  • Social media: 120 (57%)
  • Branding: 106 (51%), against 43% statewide
  • Content marketing: 85 (41%)
  • Email: 64 (31%)
  • Ecommerce: 58 (28%)
  • Video: 48 (23%)

46 web design agencies do not name SEO. If you want the site built with search in mind, ask about it directly. Only 58 name ecommerce, so if you run an online store, check for store builds in their portfolio before anything else.

Team size

189 list a team size. 167 (88%) have fewer than 50 people. The biggest groups are 10 to 49 people (67 agencies) and 2 to 9 people (60). 4 are freelancers.

Age

184 list a founding year. The median is 2014, and 81 (44%) started in 2015 or later. The oldest, MāHK Advertising and ZipLocal, date to 1971.

What they charge

107 web design agencies publish an hourly rate band. The median starting rate is $100 an hour. 74 (69%) start at $100 or more, and 32 (30%) start at $150 or more. The most common band is $100 to $149 an hour (35 agencies), then $150 to $199 (20).

124 publish a minimum project size. "$1,000+" is the most common (49), then "$1,000 to $10,000" (29) and "$5,000+" (22). 10 take projects under $1,000.

Industries

120 list the industries they serve. Home services leads with 53, then ecommerce (44), healthcare (31), dental (16), and legal and financial services (13 each).

How the data was built

Candidates came from Clutch, DesignRush and the BBB plus a hand-built list. Dead sites, non-Utah firms and video-only shops were removed. Services are matched from each agency's own homepage text, so an agency that builds sites but never says so on its homepage is not counted.
